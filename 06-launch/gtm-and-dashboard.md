# GTM Strategy & Success Dashboard (Module 6)

## Get your prior work ready
- **The feature you're launching from your M4 PRD, one feature, not a list:** Spotlight Curated Rail
- **What your M5 experiment told you shipped / iterating, the evidence behind the launch:** I believe that Spotlight Curated Rail for the Wanderer will result in Increased engagement and retention, as measured by a 4% change in Increased Search to play conversion rate and increased visitors with 30+ minute sessions within 2 months. We will protect Power User proportion at 22% throughout the test.
- **Your persona pull your M2 persona, this anchors your audience:** Priya, a 34-year-old subscriber with 14 years of tenure, one of StreamLine's most loyal, long-standing viewers. She wants to sit down and quickly land on something she actually wants to watch, without the session turning into a chore. She opens the app, scrolls for roughly twenty minutes through a catalog of 15,000 titles, finds nothing she wants, and closes the app without watching anything — in her words, she "ended up going back to a DVD."

## Set your goal, then your audience
- **Primary GTM goal awareness · engagement · conversion, pick one:** Engagement
- **Why this goal? what makes this the right goal for this feature right now:** One of the biggest issues with Streamlight is the lack of engagement and users dropping off. Therefore our right goal for this GTM is increasing engagement
- **Target audience the specific segment your goal implies, be precise:** The specific audience this goal implies is for the casual watcher or the wanderer. The person who browse for a while or do not engage in long sessions.

## Size your launch
- **Launch tier S (minimal) · M (targeted) · L (multi-channel) · XL (full GTM):** M

Not massive but the revenue impact from conversion would be via converting the target users to power users where the Average monthly LTV is $14.20 compared to $11.4 for Casual Browers and $8.4 for Wanderers.
- **Is this a launch or a release? does it need go-to-market, or can it just ship?:** Release
