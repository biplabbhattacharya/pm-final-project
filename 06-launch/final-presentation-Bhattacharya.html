Slide 05 · Strategy

Why discovery is broken for Explorers

05 / 10
Problem statement hook

Casual Explorers or Wanderers open StreamLine to unwind but bounce from a generic, algorithm-only home screen. "Nothing to play" is the #1 reason cited for short, sub-10-minute sessions and fewer 30+ minute sessions

Value proposition

Spotlight reframes discovery from endless scrolling into a curated, reasoned shortlist, six titles, each with a one-line "why you will love this" so the choice feels effortless.

“
Data-backed hypothesis

We believe a personalised Spotlight rail for Casual Explorers will increase the share of 30-minute sessions started from discovery, measured by a +4pt lift within 2 months, without power user numbers.

Slide 10 · Story

What I learned building Spotlight

10 / 10
Friction points

It was hard to decide the right persona to solve for and the right primary metric to design for.

The Aha! moment

The Aha! moment happened when I realized that one feature doesn't have to solve all problems, I just need to decide my feature based on persona, primary metric, guardrail metric and then revisit this to find other features that solve other issues.

Key takeaways

It was surprising how many times I double/triple checked if the persona, primary metric, guardrail metric was what I want to develop for.

Persona Primary metric Guardrail metric
What I would do next

Next I will design features that solve for rest of the issues surfaced through discovery
